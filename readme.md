<h2 align="center">Research summary</h2>

<p>This study develops a validation-aware weak-supervision framework for benchmarking suicidal ideation and broader mental health disclosures on Bluesky. It combines lexicon-based retrieval, Llama-3-8B weak labels, human validation, and model benchmarking across two corpora containing 8,346 and 9,988 posts, respectively. Human review reveals distinct labeling errors: weak labels miss some suicidal ideation disclosures and over-identify broader mental health disclosures. These findings show why benchmark scores require task-specific validation and careful interpretation. Validation covers stratified subsets, and the benchmarks assess post-level disclosures rather than clinical diagnoses or individual risk.</p>

<p><strong>Keywords:</strong> Suicidal ideation detection; mental health NLP; weak supervision; decentralized social media; Bluesky; label validation.</p>
